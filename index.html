Questions, answered.

Is WorkMate a CRM or an ERP?

A CRM at its core, with finance, design, HR and service built around the same customer and work records. We call it a business operating system: one shared record, a different view for every team.

Do we have to replace our accounting software?

No. Keep your accounting system as the books of record. WorkMate syncs with it and never double-posts. If you'd rather, you can also run your books natively in WorkMate.

Can we import from spreadsheets or our current CRM?

Yes. Self-service imports let you preview every row, match fields and resolve conflicts before anything is saved. Our team helps with larger migrations.

Can the AI change our data?

Not on its own. Anything that matters, like sending email, changing a deal, issuing an invoice, moving money or deleting a record, needs a person's confirmation. Every AI-assisted action is logged.

Who can see salary or HR information?

Only people with explicit access. Shared work context, like who's on leave and when, never reveals why, how much someone earns, or anything in an HR case.
